The short version

Most people building AI agents have never made software touch the physical world. For two years that is the only kind of software I have written. Not in simulation. On real hardware, shipped in front of real audiences.

I reverse-engineered the remote-pilot system behind Japan's Avatar Robot Café and rebuilt my own gesture-controlled arm in seven days. I demonstrated a robotic arm at PROMETEO 2025 at IIT Jodhpur, where a professor recommended advancing the work. When an Arduino Mega hit its wireless ceiling, I moved the entire codebase to ESP32 and kept the system shipping.

The range runs the whole stack: firmware for ESP32 and Arduino, custom UART protocols written from scratch where no library existed, DLIB compiled from source on memory-starved boards, vision models with YOLO and MediaPipe, and local language pipelines with Ollama and retrieval. Now I am pointing that foundation at agentic AI, at systems that plan, call tools and act rather than only perceive and converse.

Work

Four builds that went outside the lab.

2023 – 2025 RoboAI Hub

The intelligence layer of a public humanoid robot

Face recognition, a GPT-3.5 conversation engine and a speech-to-text and text-to-speech pipeline, all fitted onto a 1 GB Raspberry Pi. DLIB would not install, so I compiled it from source under the memory limit. The robot ran daily public demonstrations at Umaid Club and Nehru Park in Jodhpur and was covered by four regional newspapers.

  • Compute budget 1 GB Raspberry Pi
  • Ran for 22 months
  • Press 4 regional newspapers
June 2025 MBM University

KineLink, a gesture-controlled robotic arm

I studied the DAWN remote-pilot system behind Japan's Avatar Robot Café, worked out how the piloting loop held together, and built my own version in seven days. The arm reads hand gestures and mirrors them, no controller in between.

November 2024 RoboAI Hub

Servo control across the XY and XZ planes

A robotic arm that moves through two planes with smooth angle interpolation, driven by a custom UART protocol between an Arduino Nano and a Raspberry Pi. Closed-loop joint position came from potentiometer feedback. No library existed for the link, so the protocol was written from scratch. The arm was later demonstrated at PROMETEO 2025 at IIT Jodhpur.

January 2025 PM SHRI KV ISA, Mount Abu

A five-day AI and machine learning curriculum, written from nothing

Python fundamentals, core AI, computer vision and the mathematics underneath the models, structured into five days and taught to more than fifty school students. I also mentored three junior interns through embedded systems and Python.
